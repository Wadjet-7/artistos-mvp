## Product Hunt First Comment (Maker Story)

Hey everyone! I'm Larry, the founder of ArtistOS.

I built this because I saw a gap that's been frustrating artists for years: the business side of art has no dedicated tools. Artists are stuck juggling spreadsheets for invoicing, Google Docs for contracts, random apps for portfolio tracking, and posting manually to social media.

ArtistOS brings it all together in one platform:

- Portfolio management with AI-powered descriptions and pricing suggestions
- Contract generator (commission, consignment, licensing, sales)
- Invoice and expense tracking with PDF export
- Social media content scheduler
- Viewing rooms to share curated collections with collectors
- Artist CV builder with public shareable link
- Commission pipeline tracking
- Contact CRM for collectors and galleries
- Market analytics dashboard

The Starter plan is free forever — no credit card, no trial that expires. Pro is $19/mo for serious working artists, and Studio is $49/mo for unlimited everything.

I'd love your feedback. What features would make this more useful for the artists in your life?
